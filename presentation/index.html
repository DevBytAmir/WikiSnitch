    How to present

    Three keys are enough

    1. 1Go full screen

      Press Present full screen, or F on the slides. Esc leaves full screen.

    2. 2Move through it

      → or Space for the next slide, ← to go back, Home and End to jump. A click on the right or left side works too.

    3. 3Light or dark

      D switches the theme, as in the app. The PDF is always the light version.

    Changed a slide? Run node presentation/build.mjs to rebuild the PDF and the pictures above, or open the print version and save it as PDF from the browser.
