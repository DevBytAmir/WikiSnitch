What it does

Through time

Every paragraph keeps its identity across thousands of edits. See when it appeared, every change to its wording, and which edits were vandalism that was undone.

Across languages

The closest paragraph in the English and Swedish articles, how close it is, and whether the numbers agree.

Ask, with receipts

Ask in Finnish, Swedish or English. Answers are cited to paragraphs, each with a trust badge, and can be asked as of a past date.

Similarity measures meaning, not truth. WikiSnitch never claims which of two texts is right.

How it uses MariaDB

Paragraphs live in system-versioned tables, so MariaDB itself keeps every earlier state. Meaning is searched with a native VECTOR(1024) index. "What did Wikipedia say about this in 2012?" is one statement that joins the two:

SELECT v.distance, p.text
FROM ( SELECT text_hash, VEC_DISTANCE_COSINE(embedding, VEC_FromText(?)) AS distance
       FROM text_vector WHERE lang = 'fi' ORDER BY distance LIMIT 200 ) v
JOIN paragraph FOR SYSTEM_TIME AS OF TIMESTAMP ? AS p
  ON p.text_hash = v.text_hash AND p.lang = 'fi' AND p.undone = 0
ORDER BY v.distance
